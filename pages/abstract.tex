\noindent
An abstract is an outline of the results of research that
requires a brief description. The abstract is composed of short,
clear, structured, and systematic sentences so that it can
describe the whole report. It states the research title, the
research objective, the research variable boundaries, and the
analysis method used, then summarizes the results and discussion,
and finally reveals the conclusion of the research.
